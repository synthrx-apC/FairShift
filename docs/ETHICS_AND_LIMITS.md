# Ethics and limits

- All seeded records are synthetic and fictional.
- The product is decision support, not legal advice. Demo rule values are not verified law.
- No emotion detection from voice or face. No individual quit-risk score is shown.
- Anonymous reporting has limits; the demo uses pseudonyms and does not claim perfect anonymity.
- AI is not used to determine overtime or payroll numbers. Those should be deterministic calculations.
- Human review is required before acting on disputes, changing legal rules, or using workforce forecasts.
- Do not use this prototype to make employment, disciplinary, wage or safety decisions about real people.
