# Cross-sector adoption

FairShift separates a shared workflow from a sector pack. The shared workflow covers check-in receipts, pay transparency, hazard reports, issue status, team-level indicators and an audit record. Sector packs supply role names, hazard categories, pay model defaults, check-in options and workplace-specific policy settings.

- **Construction:** daily shift workers, gate QR check-in, outdoor heat exposure, equipment and PPE hazards, contractor payment visibility.
- **College campus:** housekeeping, security, maintenance and mess teams; monthly pay; facilities and campus hazards.
- **Hospital:** 24×7 nursing, sanitation and security teams. The current hospital pack is a simulated prepared draft and needs human review.
- **Factory:** can be added by defining production-line roles, shift rotations, machine hazards, certification requirements and coverage rules in a new pack.

Small employers could start with attendance and safety reporting, then add pay checks and aggregated workforce indicators. Cost assumptions, compliance claims and integrations must be validated before real deployment.
